How this image compressor works

When you choose an image, it's drawn onto an in-memory HTML canvas at your original or resized dimensions. The canvas then re-encodes the image at the quality level you choose using your browser's built-in image encoder. The result is a new file, generated entirely on your device — the original image is never sent anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't the quality slider affect PNG output?

PNG is a lossless format, so it doesn't have a quality setting the way JPEG and WebP do. To shrink a PNG further, try reducing its dimensions with the max width option, or switch to JPEG or WebP output.

Is there a file size limit?

No hard limit is enforced by the tool, though very large images may take a moment longer to process since everything runs on your device.

Does compressing an image reduce its dimensions?

Not unless you set a max width. By default the tool keeps the original width and height and only reduces file size through the quality setting.
