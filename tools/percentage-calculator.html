How to calculate a percentage

To find what X% of Y is, multiply Y by X and divide by 100. To find what percent X is of Y, divide X by Y and multiply by 100. To find a percentage increase or decrease between two numbers, subtract the starting value from the new value, divide by the starting value, then multiply by 100 — a positive result is an increase, a negative result is a decrease.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage discount?

Use "What is X% of Y?" with X as the discount percentage and Y as the original price to find the amount taken off, then subtract that from the original price.

What does a negative percentage change mean?

A negative result in the increase/decrease calculation means the value went down. For example, going from 100 to 80 is a −20% change.
