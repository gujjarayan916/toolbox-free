How the word counter works

Type or paste any text into the box above. The tool splits your text on whitespace to count words, counts every character including and excluding spaces, splits on sentence-ending punctuation to estimate sentences, and counts paragraphs by line breaks. All of this happens instantly in your browser as you type — nothing is sent anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated using an average adult silent reading speed of 200 words per minute, rounded up to the nearest minute.

Does this tool store or upload my text?

No. All counting happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never sent to a server, logged, or saved after you leave the page.

Is there a limit to how much text I can paste in?

No hard limit is enforced, though extremely large documents (hundreds of thousands of words) may feel slightly slower since counting runs on your device.
